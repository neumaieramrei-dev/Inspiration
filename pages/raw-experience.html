THE PHILOSOPHY

Before you
make sense
of it.

Raw Experience is an invitation to step out of interpretation and into attention.

We are used to looking at everything through a filter: what something means, whether we like it, whether it is useful, beautiful or worth our time.

These exercises interrupt that habit. They give you something small to experience without telling you what to create from it.

Stay with the moment before you name it. Notice what your attention does when there is nothing to achieve.

There is no result to reach.
The experience itself is the material.

THREE IMPULSES

Let something
happen.

Choose a card.
Flip it. Follow the instruction.
Don't overthink it.

01 FLIP ME
01 RAW EXPERIENCE

Sit in front of a mirror and look at your reflection. Look very closely, even at the things you haven’t yet learnt to love. Think about how organic shapes and structures from nature are reflected in your skin.

NOTICE WHAT YOU NORMALLY LOOK PAST.
02 FLIP ME
02 RAW EXPERIENCE

Lie down on the floor and stare at the ceiling whilst listening to a 20-minute Pink Floyd song.

LET TIME BECOME PART OF THE EXPERIENCE.
03 FLIP ME
03 RAW EXPERIENCE

Sit down on your own in a café and don’t take anything with you to keep yourself occupied. Watch the people going by and listen in on the conversations at the tables next to you.

BE PRESENT WITHOUT NEEDING TO PARTICIPATE.

one experience is enough.
