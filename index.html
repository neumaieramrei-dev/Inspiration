01 THE IDEA

Inspiration,
without the
overload.

We live in a world where inspiration is everywhere, and somehow, that can make it harder to be creative.

When inspiration becomes imitation

Platforms like Pinterest give us endless images, references and ideas. But when we are constantly consuming other people's work, inspiration can quickly turn into imitation.

We start with what we've seen instead of what we feel, think or imagine ourselves.

We believe in a different kind of inspiration.

Our exercises don't show you what to create. They give you something to experience.

A sound. A feeling. A limitation. A strange question. A physical challenge. A moment of attention.

From there, there is space for your own imagination to take over.

A little less. A little more.

Less visual input. More room to think.
Less scrolling. More creating.
Less imitation. More individuality.

We don't want to fill your head with ideas.

We want to give you the space
to find your own.
